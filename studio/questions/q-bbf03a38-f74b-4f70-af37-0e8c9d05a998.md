Denied: `repo/` is another checkout, and what it holds may not be the PR's lockfile. Stay in your own checkout and read the branch under review from git: `git fetch origin ticket/01-project-setup` and then `git show origin/ticket/01-project-setup:pnpm-lock.yaml | grep '@house'` (it should find nothing). For merged state, use `origin/main` the same way.
