Allowed. The command only reads files (cat, grep). After `cd apps/web`, the paths `../../smoke` and `../../scripts` point to your own 08 worktree, not to the project root. The check resolved them as if the `cd` hadn't run. To avoid this hold next time, run greps from your worktree root without a `cd`, e.g. `grep -rniE "favicon|icon" apps/web/src smoke scripts -l`.
